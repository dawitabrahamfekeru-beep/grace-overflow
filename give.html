Why it matters

Right now the church takes cash on a Sunday. That works for the room in Kampala, and reaches none of the thousands watching from Birmingham, Frankfurt, Toronto or Tel Aviv, who have no way to give at all.

Questions

Before you give

Do I have to give?
No. If you are visiting, you are our guest. Giving is for those who call this church home. Nobody will ever pass a basket in front of you.
Is Mobile Money safe?
Yes. You enter your MTN or Airtel number here, then approve the payment with your own PIN on your own phone. The church never sees your PIN and never holds your number.
Can I give from abroad?
Yes, by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay in GBP, EUR, USD, CAD, AED or ILS. Your currency is detected automatically and you can change it above.
Can I set up monthly giving?
Yes. Switch the frequency to Monthly. You can pause or cancel it yourself at any time from the link in your receipt email, without having to ask anyone.
Can I claim Gift Aid or a US tax receipt?
Not yet. That requires a registered charity in the UK and a 501(c)(3) in the US. If a meaningful share of giving comes from those countries, it is worth setting up. It adds 25% to every UK pound at no cost to the giver.
